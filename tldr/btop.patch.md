- terminal-setup extras (davetang/terminal-setup):

- Force UTF-8 when a remote terminal's detection fails (garbled boxes over ssh):

`btop --utf-force`

- Keys: f filter · + and - expand or collapse the tree · k signal a process · Esc menu · q quit:

- Settings are written on quit; edit them directly with:

`vi {{~/.config/btop/btop.conf}}`
